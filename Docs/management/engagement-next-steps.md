## Plan

| Step | Focus | Window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01 Align** | Deep dive on your estate, priorities and success criteria | 1–2 weeks |
| **02 Assess** | Run a focused discovery (Atlas) and initial disposition | 2–4 weeks |
| **03 Plan** | Define target products, waves and business case (Horizon + Verdict) | 2–4 weeks |
| **04 Execute** | Pilot first product end-to-end (Compass → Forge → Prove) and prepare for scale | 8–12 weeks |

---

## What we need from you

- **Access to stakeholders** — business, data, technology, risk and finance  
- **Sample estate scope** — systems, domains, key data sets  
- **Priority outcomes** — e.g. cost, risk, time to value, regulatory drivers  
- **Executive sponsorship** — to remove blockers and enable scale

| Theme | Meaning |
